✦ FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know before you install.

Is StudySnap free?
Yes. You get 5 captures every day for free — no credit card, no API key, no trial countdown. Need more? You can support StudySnap on Ko-fi and get monthly credits for extra captures.
Is this cheating?
StudySnap is built to help you understand, not just copy. Every answer comes with a clear "why" explanation and a confidence score so you actually learn the material — use it to check your work, study faster, and grasp tricky concepts.
Does it work on my quiz or study site?
Almost certainly. Because StudySnap reads what's on your screen, it works on virtually any site — Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, Google Forms, Quizizz, and more — even when the quiz is embedded or the text can't be copied.
Do you store my screenshots?
No. Screenshots are sent for analysis and never stored on our servers. We only keep your answer history locally, on your own device, so you can review past captures.
Do I need an OpenAI or API key?
Nope. We handle the AI for you — just sign in with Google and start capturing. No accounts to create, no keys to paste, no extra cost.
What languages does it support?
Many. StudySnap answers in the same language as your question, so it works great for English, Spanish, and other languages — perfect for study materials in your own language.
