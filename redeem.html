✓ Credits apply automatically

Your credits are handled for you

When you support StudySnap on Ko-fi using the same email you sign in to StudySnap with, your credits are added to your account automatically — just reopen the extension.

Used a different email on Ko-fi?

  1. Find your Ko-fi transaction ID (in the receipt Ko-fi emailed you).
  2. Email it to support@trystudysnap.com from your StudySnap account address, and we'll apply your credits right away.

Tip: to avoid this, use the same email on Ko-fi and StudySnap.
